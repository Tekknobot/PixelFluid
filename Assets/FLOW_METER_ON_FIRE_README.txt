SURFER SLUG - FLOW METER / ON FIRE PATCH

Flow is earned by clean landed trick chains. Singles add 10, doubles 28, triples 48.
After 2.5 seconds without a successful landing, Flow drains at 8 per second.
At 100 Flow the surfer enters ON FIRE for 10 seconds.

ON FIRE effects:
- 1.5x Stoke for landed aerial tricks
- 20% faster trick animations
- 12% stronger vertical trick lift
- pulsing orange HUD meter and countdown
- successful chains extend the timer slightly

Tune these values on Air Trick Score System under Flow Meter / On Fire.
