ANIMATED HEART MIDDLE-LANE PATCH

- Adds one six-frame animated heart to the middle inter-wave lane.
- Uses the existing health_heart_float sprite sheet.
- The heart chooses random horizontal directions and speeds.
- It wanders vertically inside the lane, bobs, tilts, flips, and remains within the shared water bounds.
- BeachGameBootstrap installs the heart automatically at runtime.

Inspector tuning is available on HeartLaneSpawner, HeartLaneDrifter, and HeartSpriteAnimation.
