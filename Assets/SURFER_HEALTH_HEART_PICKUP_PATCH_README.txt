SURFER HEALTH + HEART PICKUP PATCH

Added:
- 3-point surfer health system with inspector controls.
- Small world-space life bar above the surfer.
- Shark attacks now damage one health point instead of instantly killing.
- Brief invulnerability, white sprite flashing, scale pulse, and directional bump on hit.
- The original death response still plays when health reaches zero.
- Hearts are collectible from either wave bordering their inter-wave lane (the nearest two water rows).
- Hearts only collect when the surfer needs health.
- Heart pickup restores health and produces a green sprite flash/scale reaction on the surfer.
- The collected heart flies toward the surfer, enlarges, and fades.
- A replacement heart automatically spawns after the adjustable respawn delay.
- Replacement hearts may choose a random inter-wave lane.

Main inspector settings:
TinyWaveSurfer > Health and Hit Reaction
HeartLaneSpawner > Respawn Delay / Choose Random Lane After Pickup
HeartLaneDrifter > Pickup
SurferHealthBar > offset, size, and colours
