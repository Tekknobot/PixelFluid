Developer chapter upgrade/save patch

- Developer Next Chapter immediately grants the mechanics expected at the destination chapter before the checkpoint is written.
- Developer Spawn Boss grants all Final Wave mechanics before saving.
- Continue/load reconciles saved mechanics with the saved story stage, preventing a developer-created save from loading without its chapter abilities.
- Existing selected upgrade levels (jump, water slash, skid) are preserved and are never reset by this reconciliation.
- Day 2 and later continue to unlock the complete mastery move set.

Day 1 stage minimums:
- Dangerous Water: charged jump, handstand, item throwing
- Strange Tide: rotation, flip, double/triple chains, Flow
- Storm: water skid, water slash
- Final Wave: Flow finisher
