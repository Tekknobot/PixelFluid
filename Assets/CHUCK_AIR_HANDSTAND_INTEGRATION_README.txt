CHUCK AIR HANDSTAND INTEGRATION

During the forward obstacle surf jump:
- Press F or X on keyboard, or controller X/action, to trigger chuck_handstand.
- You may also release and press Space/controller A again while airborne.
- Airborne action input performs the handstand instead of throwing an item.
- The animation plays once, then returns to chuck_surf_jump if airtime remains.
- Landing restores chuck_move or Idle normally.
