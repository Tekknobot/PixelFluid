SURFER SLUG - GAMEPAD CAMERA CONTROLS

Y / North Button:
- Toggle the surfer cinematic camera.
- Keyboard Z still performs the same toggle.

Right Stick Up / Down:
- Smoothly zoom in and out.
- Each camera mode remembers its own manual zoom adjustment.

Right Stick Click (R3):
- Reset the current camera mode to its default zoom.

Inspector settings are on TinySurferCinematicCamera under Zoom:
- Minimum Orthographic Zoom
- Maximum Orthographic Zoom
- Gamepad Zoom Speed
- Gamepad Zoom Dead Zone
- Enable Gamepad Camera Controls
