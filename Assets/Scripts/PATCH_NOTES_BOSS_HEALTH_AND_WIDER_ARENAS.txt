Boss health and arena camera patch

- Adds a world-space health bar to Godzilla/Reaper and Rubber Duck bosses.
- Health bars read the bosses existing CurrentHealth/MaximumHealth properties.
- Bars remain tied to the boss lane/sorting and disappear on defeat.
- Arena width now defaults to 1.65 camera widths, allowing healthy camera follow movement.
- Boss and player remain constrained by the same arena boundaries.
