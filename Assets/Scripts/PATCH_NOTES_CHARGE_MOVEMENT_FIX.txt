CHARGE MOVEMENT FIX

The previous patch updated playerHorizontalVelocity while charging, but
UpdateChargedWaterSkid returned before the regular player movement block
applied that velocity to localRideX.

Now, while Allow Movement While Charging is enabled:
- Charged steering updates velocity.
- That velocity is immediately applied to localRideX.
- World-section rebinding and arena clamping still run.
- All earlier combined fixes remain included.
