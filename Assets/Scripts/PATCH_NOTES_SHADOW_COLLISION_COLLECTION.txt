SHADOW COLLISION ITEM COLLECTION

- Removed all item-attraction and item-pulling behavior from ShadowSurferEcho.
- The Shadow now has a small trigger collider.
- An ocean item is collected only when it physically touches the Shadow.
- Collection calls OceanItemBehaviour.Collect(player), which uses Chuck's
  normal CollectThrowableItem inventory path.
- The item's sprite is therefore added to the player's throwable inventory.
- Enemy attraction remains unchanged and separate from item collection.
