UNIFIED BOSS ENCOUNTER PATCH

SurfDayProgressionDirector now uses one shared BuildBossEncounter coroutine for:
- Normal Final Wave progression
- Continue/load restoration at Final Wave
- Developer Spawn Boss

The shared path:
- Applies the correct stage mechanics
- Gives the complete combat loadout only for Developer Spawn Boss
- Sets Final Wave state and synchronizes day/night
- Stops hostile spawners and retreats ordinary sea life
- Optionally clears stale bosses, minions, spawners and arenas
- Creates the correct Day 1 or Day 2 boss
- Waits for the asynchronous Rubber Duck spawner to finish
- Verifies the boss exists
- Verifies or repairs BossArenaPrison
- Applies upgrades to active players
- Saves only after the encounter is ready

DebugNextChapter was left unchanged.
