BOOMBOX CASSETTE SWITCHING

- Touch the summoned boombox with the player.
- Press D-pad Up alone to insert and play the next cassette.
- Press D-pad Down alone to insert and play the previous cassette.
- Plays cassette_insert before beginning the selected track.
- Available tracks are Death Surfer, High Life, and Wind It Up.
- Uses a trigger around the boombox and ignores AI surfers.
- Input is edge-triggered and cooldown-protected, so holding the D-pad does not rapidly skip tracks.
