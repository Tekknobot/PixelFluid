PLAYER MUSIC BOARD SUMMON

Controls:
- Xbox / controller LB: summon or release the boombox surfboard
- Keyboard M: summon or release the boombox surfboard

Behavior:
- Strange Tide unlocks the summon; it no longer forces the board to spawn.
- First press summons the board beside Chuck on Chuck's current lane.
- Music retains its spatial distance, stereo pan, and low-pass presentation.
- Second press releases it with a short audio fade, sprite fade, and drift.
- Water Slash remains RB / keyboard R.
- Existing SpawnOnce calls remain compatible for developer testing.
