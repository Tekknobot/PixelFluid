BOSS OBJECTIVE ARENA UPDATE

- Removed the automatic arena survival countdown.
- Reaper Tide escape opens after 4 accepted boss hits.
- Duck Storm is now phase-based:
  1. Destroy 4 ducklings with thrown items.
  2. The Rubber Duck becomes vulnerable for 6 seconds.
  3. Land one hit to complete the phase.
  4. Complete 3 phases to open the escape route.
- Projectiles still ricochet while either boss is armoured.
- Existing player, skid, combo, and camera arena clamps remain intact.
- Boss panel vertical position is exposed as panelY in BossArenaPrison.
