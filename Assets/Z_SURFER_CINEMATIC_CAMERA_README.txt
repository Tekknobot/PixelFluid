Z SURFER CINEMATIC CAMERA

Press Z during Play Mode to toggle the tiny-surfer cinematic camera.

When enabled:
- Finds the active TinyWaveSurfer automatically.
- Smoothly zooms toward it.
- Smoothly follows its position.
- Adds subtle horizontal and vertical movement look-ahead.
- Continues following while the surfer switches between simulations.
- Temporarily disables SmoothBoardCamera and BeachCameraFollow on the camera.

Press Z again:
- Restores the previous camera position.
- Restores the previous orthographic size or field of view.
- Re-enables the camera-follow scripts that were active before cinematic mode.

The controller is installed automatically on Camera.main at runtime.

Runtime camera object component:
    TinySurferCinematicCamera

Useful Inspector controls:
- Orthographic Zoom
- Perspective Field Of View
- Framing Offset
- Follow Smooth Time
- Horizontal Look Ahead
- Vertical Look Ahead
- Zoom Smooth Time
