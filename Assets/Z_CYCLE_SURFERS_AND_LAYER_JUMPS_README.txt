Z CAMERA CYCLING + SURFER LAYER JUMPS

CAMERA
- First Z press enables cinematic mode and zooms to Surfer 1.
- Every additional Z press focuses and zooms to the next surfer.
- After the final surfer, Z cycles back to the first surfer.
- Press Escape to leave cinematic mode and restore the normal camera.
- Camera clamping remains enabled so it does not reveal space beyond the
  focused surfer's current simulation bounds.

SURFERS
- Surfers still ride to an edge, perform a trick, turn around, and ride back.
- They now jump to different simulation layers more frequently.
- With three or more simulations, each transfer chooses a random layer that
  is different from the current one.
- Transfers use a higher visible jump and a 540-degree aerial spin.
- The jump height scales slightly with the distance between simulation layers.

NEW TINYWAVESURFER INSPECTOR SETTINGS
- Jump To Random Wave Layer
- Layer Jump Height
- Seconds Per Simulation
- Switch Duration
