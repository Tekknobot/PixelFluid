WATER SLASH / FLOW FINISHER IMPACT POLISH

- Ordinary Water Slash now creates a foam-and-droplet burst on impact.
- The slash briefly flashes white, expands, descends and fades instead of disappearing instantly.
- Flow Finisher creates a larger impact burst on every valid target while continuing to pierce.
- Both projectile types emit a lightweight foam trail.
- Projectiles naturally slow, descend and fade during the final part of their lifetime.
- Only the existing water_slash and flow_finish SFX are played. No voice line was added.
