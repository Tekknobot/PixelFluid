CHARGED WATER SKID

Controls
- Keyboard: hold E, then release.
- Xbox-style controller: hold east face button / B, then release.

Behaviour
- The surfer vibrates while charging.
- Releasing launches a wave-following skid in the surfer's facing direction.
- Hold duration controls skid speed and duration.

Inspector tuning is under TinyWaveSurfer > Charged Water Skid.
