BOOMBOX DEATH SURFER SWIMMER

- Automatically spawns one animated boombox-on-a-surfboard swimmer after the wave sections are ready.
- Uses all 16 sliced frames from Resources/Boombox/boombox.
- Roams horizontally, bobs with the water, changes inter-wave lanes, and sometimes moves toward the player's lane when nearby.
- Plays Resources/Audio/Music/Death Surfer as a looping world-space source.
- Music volume fades smoothly with player distance, stereo position shifts left/right, and distant sound is low-pass filtered.

Main tuning fields are on BoomboxSurferSwimmer:
maximumVolume, fullVolumeDistance, silentDistance, spatialBlend, stereoPanStrength, and lowPassCutoffRange.
