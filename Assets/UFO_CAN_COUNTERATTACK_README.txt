UFO CAN COUNTERATTACK PATCH

- Soda cans now accumulate as an integer inventory on TinyWaveSurfer.
- Picking up another can no longer fails when one is already held.
- Action alone throws at the nearest shark or giant squid.
- Hold Up (W/Up Arrow, D-pad Up, or Left Stick Up) and press Action
  (F/X keyboard or controller X/Square) to fire directly at the UFO.
- Upward shots do not consume a can when no visible UFO is available.
- UFO hits cancel the tractor beam immediately, force a retreat, and keep the
  ship offscreen for a configurable cooldown before it returns.
- UFO collision is configured automatically with a trigger BoxCollider2D and
  kinematic Rigidbody2D.

Inspector settings are under AlienUfoController > Can Hit Response.
