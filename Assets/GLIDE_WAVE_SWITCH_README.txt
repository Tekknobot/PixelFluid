GLIDE WAVE SWITCH
=================

New player mechanic:

1. Hold Xbox B (keyboard E or B) to charge the existing water skid.
2. Release it to begin gliding with the current push force.
3. While gliding, hold Up or Down and press Xbox B again.
   Keyboard: W/S or Up/Down plus E or B.
4. The surfer carves into the adjacent wave using a flatter transfer arc.
5. Remaining glide time, speed, and travel direction continue on the new wave.

The existing Up/Down + A/Space jump-based wave switching remains unchanged.
Edge layers continue to wrap using the existing adjacent-wave rules.
